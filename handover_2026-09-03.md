## 2 The findings a successor must not re-derive

- **The cut-in's axis is the looming rate.** EL.1's equal-weight rule is a threshold on the
  optical expansion rate; fitted directly it scores 0.113 held out (gap 0.152, inverse tau
  0.168, floor 0.118); threshold 0.034 rad/s (1.9°/s). Video beats the simulator literature's
  ordering (there inverse tau wins).
- **The gate is credited.** A fixed-horizon (3 s) lateral-clearance gate in front of the rule,
  fitted on post-onset cells, predicts the 90 pre-onset cells out of sample at 0.032 and
  improves the post-onset fit to 0.103. In the field's own lane-entry weight the same
  horizon lifts the field from 0.347 to 0.261 (first field variant to beat chance) but it
  stays far behind the gap; flag `lane_entry_horizon_s` defaults off.
- **Stage 1 on the gated looming axis** beats the deficit axis by 14.9 held-out log-likelihood
  units and predicts C1 to 0.030: it is the primary. Median level 0.032 rad/s, 80th
  percentile 0.066; the percentile's cost 0.24 s per 5 points against 0.52 s from the CI.
- **The left turn needs one axis, distance** (0.056; time 0.112; looming ties at 0.054; linear
  rule 0.054 with 81–90% weight on distance; the 50-resample bootstrap's intervals include
  zero, as expected on 18 cells). The construction note's expectation about looming's sign
  was wrong and is corrected in its §5b. Decision moment 13.5 s by assumption (B3.Q1).
- **The test track reproduces the video.** Same model: 2.45 s (track) against 2.18 s (video);
  the video population carries over to the track's cells with nothing refitted (0.200
  against the track's own 0.231, chance 0.289, floor 0.108); held-out drivers +0.20
  log-likelihood per run; within-driver spread 0.20 s against 0.86 s; rating slopes −0.31
  against −0.40. The paper's statistics reproduce from the protocol (2.17/1.49 s against
  2.26/1.50). The dread condition is parked (SetPET is not what hurried drivers faced).
- **The colleague's required-deceleration analysis** converges with ours on the speed
  exponent (0.4) and supplied the gate; its walled a_req scores 0.171 on our pipeline.

## 3 Process notes and pitfalls found this arc

- **Shell-heredoc edits with `\n` in string literals broke three scripts** (a literal newline
  lands inside the string). Use the Write and Edit tools for code edits; it is now rule 9.
- **A 200-resample cell bootstrap that refits a seven-start joint model per fold ran 2.7
  CPU-hours without finishing.** Write the main report before the bootstrap; cut the
  resamples and say so in the docstring and worklog (done: 50). Check CPU time before
  assuming a run is stuck.
- **Background agents die at the usage limit; their launched scripts may or may not.** Two
  died this arc (one mid-fit, one mid-card); the scripts were relaunched or finished by hand.
- **PowerPoint locks the deck you rebuild** if Jonas has it open: write `-v2`.
