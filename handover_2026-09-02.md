## 2 The findings a successor must not re-derive

- **The R.2 verdict is numerically solid.** Independent grid fit with closed-form lapse
  reproduces every registered number to the third decimal; cell-bootstrap interval on
  the field-minus-gap difference +0.174 to +0.222 against the 0.01 margin; the field's
  model-free rank correlation with the response is +0.14 against the gap's +0.86, so no
  fitter could rescue it (`out/cutin2_lane_gate_diagnostic.md` §1–2).
- **Why the field loses, in two parts.** The lane-entry gate (ours, 2026-08-27) suppresses
  the deficit in the 64 slow-lane-change cells at starting TTC 2–3 s, whose mean response
  is 0.75; participants are flat across lane-change duration (0.85 / 0.92 / 0.92 at TTC 2,
  CP5, DV 21) where the field gives 10 005 / 4 205 / 171. Forcing the gate to 1 recovers
  0.087 of the 0.199 deficit. The released counterfactual magnitude is 4–6 times more
  sensitive to either vehicle's speed than to gap, so it orders matched-TTC cells by
  absolute speed; the study realizes DV partly through ego speed (30.7 vs 36.5 m/s).
- **The sign slip.** "orders 14 of 24 rows in the observed direction" was wrong: the
  registered report counts rows where ρ(field, P) is *negative*; the observed direction
  for a criticality measure is positive, so the field orders 10 of 24 like the data.
  Corrected in `docs/r2_gate_decisions.md` §1 and in `build_talk.py` slide 36; the built
  60-minute deck still shows the old wording (TALK.Q2, REV.Q2).
- **Surprise is an operator on a reference.** With a free reference, residual information
  reproduces any threshold model (h of exp(−s) is s), so scenario-agnosticism belongs to
  the reference. Two references qualify: a predictive model of other agents (zero by
  construction on the cyclist overtake, so not the axis; a pace-independent onset) and a
  population distribution of accepted states, which is the ellipse (residual information
  under a Gaussian is half the squared Mahalanobis distance). Proposed framing: surprise
  defines the onset, a population percentile the level (Q5.Q1).
- **The ellipse's scale is confounded with the level** on designed stimuli; the study data
  identify its shape and the per-driver ordering, not the absolute joint percentile,
  which needs a population reference from naturalistic data (EL.Q1–Q3).
- **LTAP separates time from distance by construction.** Ego script identical across all
  18 traces (turn onset 13.9–14.1 s, 6.5–6.9 m/s); oncoming at 13.9 or 19.4 m/s; at
  matched design PET the oncoming's time-to-arrival is matched (2.5–6.7 s) and its
  distance differs by 1.4×. A time-only prediction errs by −0.20 (RMS 0.21); a
  distance-only reading gets to RMS 0.08 but the 70 km/h cells still intervene 0.06 less:
  distance with a speed residual (`out/ltap_geometry.md`). Roles by yaw span. The Button
  LTAP clips (0–13.5 s) end before the turn; presses cluster at 12.6–12.9 s.
- **Card EL.1: a second axis earns its place on the cut-in.** Linear rule in log gap
  and log TTC 0.1137 held out (quadratic 0.1148) against the 1D log gap's 0.1522 and a
  conservative noise floor of 0.1176; weight on log gap 0.47–0.51 in every fold, so the
  rule is a threshold on the geometric mean of gap and TTC. Parsimony keeps the linear
  rule. The registered fitter needed a finite-objective guard for the quadratic form
  (`fit_reg` in `cutin2_two_axis.py`); on the 1D covariates it reproduces the registered
  numbers exactly (0.1522, 0.1679). Model (e) (CAMP-style axes) was re-run after an
  ego-speed bug; see the report for its number.

## 3 Process notes and pitfalls found this arc

- **The tests are scripts, not a pytest suite.** `python tests/test_X.py` counts property
  checks (31 + 33 + 40 + 87 = 191); `pytest` collects only the 35 wrapper functions. Run
  the scripts.
- **Long shell heredocs with mixed quotes fail to parse in this environment.** Write the
  text to a file with the Write tool and run a Python script that applies the edits
  (`scratchpad/apply_ellipse_edits.py` is the pattern); commit with `git commit -F`.
- **`R.fit` returns `None` when every start's objective is NaN** (a covariate with cells
  exactly at a corner and σ → 0 gives 0/0). `cutin2_two_axis.py` carries a
  finite-objective copy of the registered fitter (`fit_reg`) for that reason; the
  registered script itself is untouched.
- **The 60-minute deck's mtime is later than its build script** (08:35 vs 07:47 on
  09-01), so it may carry a hand save; it was not rebuilt (TALK.Q2). The short deck was
  built fresh and has no hand edits as of this handover.
- **Two documents mis-dated 09-01 were corrected to 09-02** in de48f68; the session
  spanned midnight.
- **Rendering decks:** PowerPoint COM on a *copy* in the scratchpad, never `$ppt.Quit()`
  (the skill's recipe); 13 PNGs per render, inspect every one.
