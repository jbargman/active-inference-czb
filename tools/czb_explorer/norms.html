The fan of imagined futures (4 s ahead)

What you are looking at. Purple: trajectories sampled with the norm tournament — at every step, propose N candidate moves, score each by compliance now, one step ahead, and H steps ahead (the worse of "now" and the future average wins — the trust cap), then draw one by lottery. Grey: the same noise with the bias off. Dashed lines: lane extent (teal) and the marginal band (pink).

Three things to try. (1) Start at lane center: the biased fan hugs the lane while raw noise sprays — this is why the model driver stays relaxed behind a well-behaved lead. (2) Drag the start past the marginal band: the shared "now" score caps every candidate, the lottery goes near-uniform, and the fan opens like raw noise — trust revoked on evidence. (3) Watch the violator's purple fan lean back toward the lane: hypotheses that re-enter the normal region regain their bias and are recaptured — the swarm expects violators to eventually return to normality.

What this is not. An illustrative lateral-only reimplementation of the real sampling rule (src/common/dynamics.py::forward_tar_agent in the authors' code) with simplified dynamics — for intuition, not for numbers. Its qualitative properties are checked by test_norms.mjs.

Rear-end norm geometry (lane 3.65 m, vehicle 1.72 m); weights as shipped: marginal 10⁻³, gross 10⁻³·10⁻². Handbook chapter 06 has the mechanism in full; chapter 10 discusses estimating these weights from data.
