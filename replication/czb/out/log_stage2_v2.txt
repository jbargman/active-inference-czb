**Specification check**: the in-sample RMSE (0.185) against the stage-0 static two-parameter probit's 0.125 on the same cells. An accumulator that fits worse in sample than the simpler model it extends is misspecified, not refuted -- that is what disqualified the v1 run (in-sample 0.182), whose noise accumulated through the 15.1 s pre-onset window (sd of the noise-alone running maximum ~3.9, comparable to the entire post-onset drift; worklog query A.3.Q1). In this v2 model the noise is gated at onset, so the pre-onset window's length -- a property of stimulus presentation, not of drivers -- no longer enters the model at all; the C1 cells are carried by the lapse floor exactly as in stage 1. The reserve remedy, a leaky accumulator, remains unexercised: it would also bound pre-onset noise but additionally discounts early post-onset evidence, altering the time-integration claim under test, and costs a free parameter.
