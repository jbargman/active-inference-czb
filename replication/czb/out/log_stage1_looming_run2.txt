## 7 Why no cross-scenario transfer test here (card B.4)

The transfer test is deliberately NOT attempted on this axis: the cyclist-overtake stimuli hold the looming rate essentially constant across their clearance conditions, which are varied laterally, so a looming axis has nothing to order them by and a null result would measure the stimulus design rather than the rule. Transfer on a per-scenario form of the boundary is card EL.2's job, and this run neither anticipates nor pre-empts it.
