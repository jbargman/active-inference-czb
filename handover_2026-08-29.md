## 1 The verdict that reorders everything else

**The field's kinematic content is ruled out on its own scenario, by a pre-registered
test.** The comparison (`replication/czb/cutin2_field_vs_gap.py`, models, folds and
decision rule committed before the run at commit e9203c6; report
`out/cutin2_field_vs_gap.md`): on the second cut-in study's 288 post-onset video
cells, with matched three-parameter threshold models on identical
leave-one-starting-TTC-out folds, the field scores held-out wRMSE **0.347** — worse
than chance (0.320) — against the log-gap threshold's **0.152** (sampling-noise floor
0.118), TTC 0.168, required deceleration 0.289. Robust to excluding the 20 non-modal
attention-check participants and to a post-hoc trace-noise repair (section 5 of the
report). The pre-registered rule fired: per the card's own pre-commitment, the
project's claim is restated around what survives.

**What survives** (gate record §4): the one-scalar **trait** claim (~69% of reliable
per-driver signal shared across all four scenarios, field-free); the boundary
distribution on its own scale (A.2.v2 moved the 50th/80th/95th percentiles by under
1%); the freed-lapse transfer (re-run this arc: 0.136 against chance 0.158, ceiling
0.120); and the finding that the best within-scenario axis is the longitudinal gap.
The elliptical/2D comparator program moves from fallback to primary for the axis
question. Program-level consequences are Jonas's: queries R2.Q4 (B.2/B.3 re-scope),
R2.Q5 (positioning), R2.Q6 (handbook correcting note).

## 2 The C1 defect: closed, and its interpretation revised

B2.Q1 was a **covariate-window error**, not a field or data error
(`out/c1_covariate_defect.md`): the C1 lookup included the manoeuvre-onset frame,
where the lane-entry projection's TTC lever arm turns ≤ 6 cm of lateral motion into
backwards-ordered near-full overlap (p_lane 0.000 / 0.318 / 0.997); and the running
max accumulated from the trace start, ~5–16 s before the shown clip (a startup blip
floored every 1.5 m overtake cell at 230.9). Fix in `comfortzone.czb_data`:
shown-window accumulation (`RANDOM_CLIP_LEAD_S` = 10 s; the Button clips' documented
start) and a C1 endpoint at −0.15 s. Cut-in C1 covariates fall to ~1 (noise, no
longer ordered); C2–C6 bit-identical; eight property tests added (suite 183 → 191).
**C1 stays in the fit and identifies the lapse.**

The revision: study 2's 90 CP1 cells are **flat** (0.000–0.115, ρ(gap) = −0.04, gaps
3.9–82 m), so drivers do not respond to adjacent-lane proximity pre-encroachment, and
study 1's C1 gradient is better read as exposure-driven anticipation (the second
study's repeat manipulation shows the learning directly: 0.547 → 0.575). B2.Q5 was
amended and resolved accordingly.

## 3 Card A.2.v2: the regeneration, and what it did not change

Run as committed plus the correlated-effects variant (`out/stage1_summary.md`,
28 min). Under the full new specification (hierarchical lapse, k = 12, shown-window
covariates, product-grid LOPO): median **5 400**, σ_pop **0.200**, percentiles moved
+0.9% / +0.2% / −0.5% — the k = 12 shift and the covariate fix largely offset, inside
the card's expected band. Two things did move: the product-grid LOPO now separates
the bias variants **decisively** (hier +52.7 units, was +0.6 under the diagonal
defect), and the ordered comfort/dread levels shifted (3 703 → 3 418, 6 397 → 6 572;
query A2v2.Q3). The correlated variant converged: **ρ = −0.717 (SE 0.128)** against
the −0.27 artifact baseline — partly a real trait — moving the 80th percentile 17
units against the pre-stated ~254 escalation threshold, so the hierarchical variant
stands as primary. The k = 12 sweep re-run under the new covariates keeps its optimum
at k = 12 (A2v2.Q1), so Jonas's constant survives its evidence base changing.

## 4 The sign derivation, and why no lateral term was built

R.2.Q1's instruction (derive before implementing) was followed:
`replication/czb/expected_deficit_sign.py`, on the actual preference function over
the study's own delta-velocity range. The point field's within-row direction at
matched TTC is **inconsistent** (ρ +1.00 at TTC 2–3 s, −1.00 at TTC 5–7 s, through
the counterfactual-residual term) where the data are uniformly negative, and
distance-scaled noise shifts E[d] by ≤ ~10% with inconsistent sign. The
expected-deficit mechanism fails for the distance effect; its lateral direction is
right but grafting it onto a rejected longitudinal core was declined (gate §3). The
DRF becomes a comparator, not a donor. The note's pre-registered LTAP/overtake tests
were cancelled, with a dated note in `docs/lateral_and_uncertainty_note.md` §6.

A process note a successor should see: the first version of that script hard-coded
its expected reading and ran the grid outside the study's physical range; the numbers
contradicted the prose. It was rewritten with the interpretation computed from the
verdict rows and the grid restricted to the design range — the same failure mode the
register's R.2.Q1 entry documents from 2026-08-28, caught the same way.

## 7 Caveats on this file

The field-versus-gap verdict is about the **longitudinal** kinematic content of the
field as fitted (with the lane gate engaged and k = 12); it does not test the lateral
machinery, which failed separately on the overtake (B.1), nor does it rule out a
differently-constructed demand measure — though required deceleration's +0.238/−0.16
showing makes that unpromising on this data. The 0.01 RMSE margin in the decision
rule was inherited from B.1.Q4's precedent, not derived from a power analysis; at the
observed +0.195 the margin is irrelevant. The CP1 flatness argument in §2 compares
across studies with different populations (43 lab-style versus 144 crowdsourced
participants) — treat it as strong, not conclusive.
